About

Process help first. Microsoft 365 and Dynamics 365 are the specialty, not the limit.

Hi, I'm Neil.

I'm a Power Platform developer based in Washington state. I've spent my career helping offices replace email chains, spreadsheets and re-typing with processes that run on their own: approval routing, document control, automatic alerts and dashboards that show where things stand.

Office Tune-Up Consulting brings that work to small and mid-sized businesses. I work with office managers and owners, not just IT, so everything comes in plain language and your team gets notes they can actually use.

What I believe

  • Process before tools. Automating a confusing process just makes the confusion faster.
  • Use what you already have. Most offices already pay for more than they use.
  • No surprises. Fixed prices, written assumptions, and any income from a recommendation disclosed up front.
  • You own it. Every project ends with a walkthrough and plain-language notes.

What I work with

Power Apps, Power Automate, Dataverse, Dynamics 365, SharePoint, Power BI and Azure Functions. Process advice applies to any tool; the hands-on building is in Microsoft 365 and Dynamics 365.
