Real-world project

Permits stall and nobody notices

A permit scorecard that shows where every job is stuck

The situation

Before a modular home can be built, the job has to be permitted. Permitting moves through several stages, and some of them are spent waiting on someone else.

Without a summary, it was hard to see how many jobs were sitting in each stage, or which ones needed a follow-up.

What changed

A scorecard counts the jobs in every permitting stage across the top of the page, from new permit through review to pre-issuance. A large number in a waiting stage shows exactly where follow-up is needed.

The same page shows the jobs and modules permitted so far this year. It can be filtered by permit technician, month and quarter, each technician has a page of their own jobs, and further pages show upcoming completion dates and how this year compares with past years.

Before

  1. Status kept in each job record
  2. Ask around to find stuck jobs
  3. Delays noticed late

After

  1. Counts by stage on one page
  2. Waiting stages stand out
  3. Follow up before it slips

What it meant for the team

  • Managers see the bottleneck at a glance.
  • Each permit technician has their own list of jobs to work.
  • This year's permitting can be compared with past years.

Built with: Power BI
