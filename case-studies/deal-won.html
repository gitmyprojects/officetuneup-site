Real-world project

Winning a deal means setting it up in several systems

Marking a deal as won sets everything up

The situation

Winning a deal started a string of setup work: the customer and job had to be created in the accounting system, folders made, the team told, the deposit invoiced and permitting started.

Each step depended on someone remembering to do it.

What changed

Marking a deal as won now starts all of it automatically. The customer is matched or created in the accounting system and the new job is set up. Job folders and a planning channel for the field team are created.

The customer gets a thank-you email, the office hears about the win, accounting is reminded to invoice the permit deposit, and permitting is assigned its next task. If any part of the job setup fails, IT is notified with the details.

Before

  1. Deal marked won
  2. Customer and job typed into accounting
  3. Each team emailed, one by one

After

  1. Deal marked won
  2. Customer, job and folders created
  3. Every team notified automatically

What it meant for the team

  • Nobody retypes the customer and job into a second system.
  • No setup step is forgotten after a busy sales week.
  • The customer hears from the company the same day they sign.

Built with: Dynamics 365 Sales, Dynamics 365 Business Central, Power Automate, SharePoint, Teams, Outlook
