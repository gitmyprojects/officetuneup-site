Real-world project

Every salesperson runs deals their own way

A guided sales path, stage by stage

The situation

Every deal moved through the same stages, from first contact to a signed contract, but what got collected along the way depended on who was handling it.

Steps were missed, and customers didn't always get the right information for their property.

What changed

Each deal now follows a guided path: Qualify, Property Evaluation, Develop, Propose and Close. Each stage asks for the information it needs before the deal can move on, such as the property's jurisdiction, zoning and parcel number, or the customer's financing and timeline.

From the Property Evaluation stage, one click emails the customer property information specific to their jurisdiction and zone, and the date it was sent is recorded on the deal.

Before

  1. Notes kept however each person likes
  2. Key details missed
  3. Customer gets general information

After

  1. Guided stages on every deal
  2. Required details at each stage
  3. Local information emailed in one click

What it meant for the team

  • Every deal has the same information at the same point.
  • Proposals and contracts start from a complete record.
  • There's a record of what each customer was sent, and when.

Built with: Dynamics 365 Sales, Power Automate
