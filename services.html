Services

Fixed prices, clear scope, and plain language throughout.

Office Tune-Up Consulting helps small and mid-sized businesses run more efficiently, grow without chaos, and keep everyone accountable. If your team is chasing approvals by email, hunting for the latest version of a document, typing the same information into three places, or finding out about problems too late, the fix starts with the process. Then your tools do the work.

I start with what you already pay for and recommend upgrades only when they clearly pay for themselves, and if I'd earn anything from a recommendation, you'll know up front. Microsoft 365 and Dynamics 365 are my specialty.

A typical tune-up begins with a short look at how your office actually works, followed by a fixed-price plan: clearer processes, approval routing, organized document storage, automatic alerts and reminders, and simple dashboards that show who's doing what. When it's done, your team gets a hands-on walkthrough and plain-language notes, and an optional ongoing maintenance plan keeps things running smoothly as you grow.

Ways to work together

Two ways to start: a Quick Fix for one problem, or a free Tune-Up Check for the whole office.
