Real-world project

Contracts go out with mistakes

Contracts built from the sales record, not by hand

The situation

Customer contracts started from a master Word document. Staff copied it, typed in the customer and job details, and edited the terms as they went. Word's track changes showed some edits, but not all of them were captured, and anyone with access to the master could change it.

Mistakes surfaced only when a customer noticed them.

What changed

A Generate PDF Document button now sits on the sales record. One click pulls the customer, address, job site and project details already entered there and fills them into an approved contract template.

The right contract package is chosen automatically by the state the project is in (Oregon or Washington), and the finished contract arrives as a PDF, ready to send.

Before

  1. Copy the master Word file
  2. Type in customer and job details
  3. Edit the terms by hand
  4. Customer spots the mistake

After

  1. Details entered once in the sales record
  2. Click Generate PDF Document
  3. Correct state's contract filled in
  4. PDF ready to send

What it meant for the team

  • Details are entered once, in the sales record, instead of being retyped into each contract.
  • Every contract starts from the same approved wording. Changing the terms means deliberately updating the template, not editing a copy.
  • Nobody has to remember which state's version to use.

Built with: Dynamics 365 Sales, Power Automate, Word templates
