Demo company

Approvals sit in someone's inbox

Approvals that route themselves

The situation

At Lumen Photonics, every change to how the business runs needs sign-off from the person who owns that process. Changes that affect the financials also need a review from the controller.

Done by email, nobody can easily tell who has a request, who approved it, or whether the right people were asked at all.

What changed

Submitting a request now sends it to the right approver automatically, based on their role rather than a name written into the setup. Financial changes go on to the controller.

One person can't approve their own request or approve it twice; if that would happen, it goes to their manager instead. Every decision is recorded with who made it, and the outcome is written back to the request.

Before

  1. Email the request
  2. Wait and follow up
  3. Search the inbox for who approved

After

  1. Submit the request
  2. Sent to the process owner
  3. Financial review if needed
  4. Decision recorded on the request

What it meant for the team

  • Requesters can see where their request stands without asking.
  • When someone changes roles, approvals follow the role. Nothing has to be rebuilt.
  • There's a clear record of every approval if an auditor asks.

Built with: SharePoint, Power Automate, Microsoft 365 groups
