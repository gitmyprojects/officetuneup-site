People skip steps in the process

Rules that can't be skipped, and an alert if the check fails

The situation

At Lumen Photonics, a change request's route depends on a few key details: whether it affects the financials, and who owns the process it changes.

If the person submitting could set those details themselves, they could skip the financial review or pick a friendly approver. Hiding the fields on the form only stops honest mistakes.

What changed

Every new request is checked automatically the moment it's created. The key details are reset to safe values: the request starts as Submitted, it's treated as affecting the financials until Business Systems reviews it, and the approver comes from the department's assigned process owner.

If the check ever fails, it doesn't fail quietly. An alert goes to Teams with the request, the reason and the step that failed.

Before

  1. Requester fills in every field
  2. Picks their own approver
  3. Financial review skipped

After

  1. Request submitted
  2. Key details reset automatically
  3. Right approver assigned
  4. Teams alert if the check fails

What it meant for the team

  • Nobody can route around the review, by accident or on purpose.
  • Requesters only fill in what they actually need to.
  • A failed check is noticed the same day, not at the next audit.

Built with: SharePoint, Power Automate, Teams
