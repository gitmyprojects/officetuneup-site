Real-world project

Crews don't know what's coming up next

The production schedule on every crew member's phone

The situation

Field and production crews needed to know which jobs were coming up, but the production schedule lived in the office's systems. Finding out meant asking someone.

What changed

A Production Queue screen in the company app shows jobs completed in the last two weeks, jobs in progress, and jobs planned for the next 28 days, in order of start date.

Tapping a job shows its queue number, job number, description and estimated start date.

Before

  1. Ask the office what's next
  2. Wait for an answer
  3. Plan on short notice

After

  1. Open the app
  2. See recent, current and upcoming jobs
  3. Prepare for what's next

What it meant for the team

  • Crews see the schedule for themselves.
  • Fewer calls to the office asking what's next.
  • Teams can get ready before a job starts.

Built with: Power Apps, SharePoint
