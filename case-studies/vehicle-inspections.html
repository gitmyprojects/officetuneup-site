Real-world project

Vehicle inspections are still on paper

Inspections on a phone, with problems sent straight to maintenance

The situation

Paper inspection checklists are easy to half-finish, and a problem written on one doesn't reach maintenance until someone hands the sheet in.

What changed

Drivers pick their vehicle from the company's vehicle list in the app, and that vehicle's checklist loads. Each item is marked satisfactory, unsatisfactory or not applicable, with a shortcut to mark everything satisfactory and adjust from there.

The inspection can't be submitted until every item is marked, every problem has a note, and the driver has signed. If anything is marked unsatisfactory, a work order listing those items is created automatically, and the maintenance technician is notified in Teams.

Before

  1. Paper checklist in the truck
  2. Problem noted, maybe
  3. Maintenance finds out later

After

  1. Checklist on the phone
  2. A note required for every problem
  3. Work order sent to maintenance

What it meant for the team

  • Every submitted inspection is complete and signed.
  • Maintenance hears about a problem as soon as the inspection is submitted, with the details already written down.
  • There's a record of every inspection for every vehicle.
