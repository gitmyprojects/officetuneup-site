Real-world project

Employees can't find the latest policy or form

Company news and documents in one app

The situation

Announcements, policy changes, benefits information and forms like W-9s reached employees in different ways, and the current version of a handbook or form wasn't always easy to find.

What changed

The company app now has an Announcements screen and an Employee Documents screen. Employees see the latest announcements in a list and can open attachments such as newsletters, benefits documents or policy updates.

Documents are stored in SharePoint, but only the ones HR has marked as ready appear in the app, so drafts never show up. HR and approved staff publish announcements and documents from an admin screen in the app, without anyone having to change the app itself.

Before

  1. Announcement sent by email
  2. Forms passed around
  3. Which handbook is current?

After

  1. HR posts it in the app
  2. Only approved documents appear
  3. Employees find it on their phone

What it meant for the team

  • Employees have one place to look for news, policies and forms.
  • HR publishes updates without waiting on IT.
  • Drafts stay hidden until they're ready.

Built with: Power Apps, SharePoint
