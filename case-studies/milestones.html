Real-world project

Reviews and birthdays get forgotten

Automatic reminders for birthdays and reviews

The situation

Birthdays, annual reviews and 30-day reviews for new hires depended on HR or a manager remembering the date, then finding the right form.

What changed

An automated check runs on a schedule against the employee records in payroll. When someone has a birthday, an annual review coming up, or reaches 30 days since their hire date, the right people get a Teams message or an email.

Review reminders come with the right form attached, pulled from one central library that holds the annual review form in English and Spanish and the 30-day review form.

Before

  1. Keep dates on a calendar
  2. Remember to check it
  3. Hunt for the current form

After

  1. Dates checked automatically
  2. Reminder sent to the right people
  3. Current form attached

What it meant for the team

  • Nobody has to track employee dates by hand.
  • Reviews happen on time, with the current form.
  • Birthdays and milestones are recognized consistently.

Built with: Power Automate, SharePoint, Teams
