Real-world project

I can't see where the labor hours went

Labor hours by job and task, for any date range

The situation

Every payroll hour was posted to a job and task in the accounting system. Answering simple questions, like which jobs used the most labor or which tasks take the longest, still meant exporting data and building a spreadsheet.

What changed

A report answers four questions, one per page: how many hours went to each job, where one job's hours went by task, which tasks take the most labor across all jobs, and how the same task compares from one job to another. Any date range or payroll week can be selected.

A short guide page explains that hours appear on the day payroll posts, so a midweek spike isn't mistaken for a busy day, and shows how to drill into the rows behind any bar and export them to Excel.

Before

  1. Export payroll postings
  2. Build a spreadsheet
  3. Answer one question

After

  1. Pick a date range or a job
  2. See hours by job and task
  3. Drill in or export to Excel

What it meant for the team

  • Project managers and accounting see where the hours went without building a spreadsheet.
  • Real hours by task are there to check the next estimate against.
  • A plain-language guide means people use the report correctly.

Built with: Power BI, Dynamics 365 Business Central
