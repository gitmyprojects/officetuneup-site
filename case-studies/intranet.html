Company news gets lost in email

One home page for the whole company

The situation

At Lumen Photonics, there was no front door. The company's main SharePoint site still showed Microsoft's sample content, and department sites could only be reached by a direct link.

Production line status traveled by phone and radio, so planners and Finance heard about downtime late.

What changed

Lumen Hub is now the company's home page in SharePoint and Teams. It shows a countdown to month-end close, the current announcement, a live status board for every production line, buttons into the change request process, and news and events from the department sites.

Every section has an owner who keeps it current. Supervisors update line status in a simple list, and everyone sees the board.

Before

  1. Updates scattered across email
  2. Line status by phone and radio
  3. Downtime heard about late

After

  1. One home page for everyone
  2. Supervisors update line status
  3. Everyone sees it right away

What it meant for the team

  • Everyone starts their day in the same place.
  • Downtime is visible as soon as a supervisor updates it.
  • Department sites share one navigation, so they're easy to find.

Built with: SharePoint, Microsoft Lists
